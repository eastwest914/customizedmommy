Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries regarding our Mommy Makeover procedures in Melville, NY.

1. What is included in a Mommy Makeover surgery?

A standard Mommy Makeover is a combination of cosmetic procedures tailored to your needs. It typically includes a tummy tuck (abdominoplasty), breast lift or enhancement, and targeted liposuction to address areas affected by pregnancy.

2. When is the best time to get a Mommy Makeover after pregnancy?

It is generally recommended to wait at least 6 months after childbirth or until you have fully finished breastfeeding and reached a stable weight before undergoing a body reshaping procedure.

3. How long is the recovery period for post pregnancy body contouring?

Most patients take 2 to 3 weeks off from daily activities and non-strenuous work. Full healing and physical exercise resumption typically take around 6 to 8 weeks depending on the extent of your procedures.

4. Will I have noticeable scars after the surgical procedure?

Incision placements are strategically made in discreet areas, such as along natural body folds or below the bikini line, to ensure scars remain hidden under standard clothing and swimwear.
